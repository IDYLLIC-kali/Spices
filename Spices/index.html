About us

More than just spices

Spice 'n' Barrels started with one idea: everyday cooking deserves real flavour, not shortcuts. We source quality ingredients, blend everything by hand in small batches, and taste every batch before it's packed.

No two harvests taste exactly the same — and we like it that way. It means your pilau, your choma and your Sunday stew get the flavour that makes them unforgettable.
